## stockproof 0.3.0 · 有据

First public release. An LLM reads stock news, earnings reports and company announcements with every point linked to its source, and a research toolkit checks each signal against 10 years of point-in-time prices. US stocks, China A-shares and Hong Kong; English and 中文.

**What's inside**
- **Daily watching**: relevance-filtered news mood, earnings read by the LLM with the earnings surprise (SUE), A-share/HK announcement monitor, "why did it move?" with citations, report-to-report comparison, daily digest, earnings calendar.
- **Screen & evidence scorecards**: whole-market A-share shortlist with reasons; scorecards where only dimensions that passed history tests count toward the total.
- **Quant lab**: backtest any idea (earnings, 业绩预告, 新闻联播 policy signals, factors) with costs and price limits; the last 2 years stay locked until you confirm once, and every variant you try raises the significance bar.
- **Portfolio check-up**: concentration, holdings that move together, risk sources, and replays of past crashes on today's weights.
- **Web dashboard** (home, stocks, digest, screen, lab, portfolio, research) and a **DeepSeek Harness / MCP plugin** with 23 tools and 10 `/stockproof-…` commands.
- Daily automatic refresh on **macOS, Windows and Linux**; tested on all three.

**Try it** (offline demo, no API key):
```bash
pip install "git+https://github.com/taoqizhang55-code/stockproof"
stockproof demo
```

Formerly named *marketmood*: old data folders, settings and DSH installs are migrated automatically.

Not investment advice.
