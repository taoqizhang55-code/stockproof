## stockproof 0.4.0 · 有据

From reading the evidence to acting on it, and every action is recorded. Every new feature is in the CLI, the web dashboard and the DeepSeek Harness / MCP plugin.

**New**
- **Ask in one sentence** (`ask`): plain words in Chinese or English go to the matching feature.
- **Charts**: candles, moving averages, MACD/RSI/KDJ, support/resistance zones. Click a candle to see that day's move, volume, news and filings. Breakout scanner.
- **Smart-money map** (`moneymap`): 43 quarters of top-10 holders, including the national team, social security, pensions, northbound, insurance, QFII and funds. Exits the disclosures hide are inferred, and daily ETF creations flag likely rescue buying. Also shown on each stock's page.
- **AI research team** (`team`): five cited analysts debate and a portfolio manager decides. Each call is scored after its horizon, so the team builds a track record.
- **Paper trading** (`paper`): A-share, HK and US accounts with real frictions.
  - Fills at the next open; limit-up/down and suspensions block trades; a 5%-of-volume cap; T+1; lots; commission and stamp duty.
  - Accounts can follow a lab strategy, the forecaster or the long-term plan, rebalanced each night.
  - Records are append-only. Accounts are archived, never deleted.
- **Price alerts** (`alerts`): on trading days only, with A-share, HK and US holidays. Live while the dashboard is open.
- **3–12 month forecaster** (`forecast`): whole-market, refit monthly. Its test status is shown next to every number.
- **Long-term plan** (`plan`): RMB and USD risk profiles, this month's buys tilted by valuation, yearly rebalancing, and 20 years of history.
- **Share cards** (`card`): team calls, paper accounts and lab results.
- 40 MCP tools; ~280 offline tests.

**What the new research found**
- **Following disclosed national-team, pension or fund buying doesn't pay.** Against other stocks disclosed in the same month, none of 24 signals clears the multiple-testing bar.
- **Next quarter's national-team buys are predictable but not profitable.** Picks are hit at 2.1× the base rate; after costs they return −0.3% a quarter.
- **The forecaster passed 2010–24 (+6.7%/yr at 6 months, t 2.8) and failed in the locked 2024-11→2026 years.** It is labelled that way in the app.
- **Diversification works.** A balanced RMB plan started at the 2007 top earned +6.7%/yr (worst drop −30%). The CSI 300 alone earned −1.7%/yr (worst drop −71%).

**Upgrade**
```bash
pip install -U "stockproof[cn,mcp]"
stockproof mcp --install-dsh
```
